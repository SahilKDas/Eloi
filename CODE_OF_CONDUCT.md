# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Eloi welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be
respectful, constructive, patient, and specific. Assume good faith, criticize
ideas and code rather than people, and make room for others to participate.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them openly;
- respecting different skill levels, identities, and viewpoints;
- keeping disagreements focused on evidence and the project;
- helping maintain a harassment-free community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing
another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates this standard. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing private or
sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and discreetly as
practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in Eloi's repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing the project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant,
version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
